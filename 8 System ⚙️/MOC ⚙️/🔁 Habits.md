## ✏️ My habits
_This table **is** your habit tracker. Add, remove or change rows and Home, your daily notes and weekly notes update on their own._

| Habit              | Property | Goal   | Days        | Minimum |
| ------------------ | -------- | ------ | ----------- | ------- |
| 🌅 Wake up early   | wake     |        |             | ✅       |

| 👟 Steps           | steps    | ≥ 6000 |             |         |


### How to fill it in
- **Habit**: an emoji, then a short name. The emoji becomes its icon.
- **Property**: where the habit is saved in your daily notes. Leave it empty and it's made from the name. ⚠️ Once you've started tracking, **don't change it**, or the old days stop counting. You *can* rename the Habit freely.
- **Goal**: leave empty for a ✅ tick-box habit. Fill it in for a habit you track with a number: `≥ 6000` (at least), `≤ 3h` (at most). The unit after the number (`h`, `km`, `min`…) is shown next to it.
- **Days**: leave empty for every day. Or list the days: `Mon Wed Fri`, `Mon-Fri`, `weekdays`, `weekends`. On other days the habit doesn't count as missed.
- **Minimum**: put a ✅ on the few habits that make a day "good enough". When those are done, the day gets its ✅ Minimum Day. Mark none and every habit counts.
- **Remove a habit**: delete its row. Your old data stays in the daily notes.

## 📏 How it works
- **Log**: on 🏠 Home click a square in the week grid, or tap the chips here or in today's daily note (works on the phone too). Number habits: type the number (`5200` or `5.2k` · `3.5` or `3h30`). The chip turns green when you hit the goal.
- **Forgot a day?** Click its square in the week grid.
- **Never miss twice**: after a missed day Home shows a ⚠️ banner and the only goal is the Minimum Day. Two misses in a row → 🧯 rescue day.
- **🌙 Tomorrow's Top 3**: write 3 things in tonight's daily note and they show up on Home tomorrow morning.
- **Sunday**: open the week's note (Calendar → week number) for the week grid and a 15-minute review.
