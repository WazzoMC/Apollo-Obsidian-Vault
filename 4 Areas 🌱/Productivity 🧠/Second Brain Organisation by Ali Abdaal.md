## What is a second brain and why you should use it
- a place where you save information: **internal and external**
- internal info = ideas 💡, brainstorms, etc
- external info = podcasts, vids, books, etc

- “[[your brain is for having ideas, not for holding them]]”

## C.O.D.E
### Capture
- capture internal and external ideas
***Example of things you can capture:***
- Highlights from a book
- an interesting quote 
- a website
- notes from a meeting
- **thoughts 💭**(you can use these for books, vids, courses you make)

### Organise
- we don’t have time to revisit stuff that we think is useful, such as “book notes folder”
- Organise based on **ACTIONABILITY** / projects, not topics

### Distill
- Highlight, summarize, bold
- make it understandable quickly at the top of the note, but still keep the old long details

### Express
- COD helps us express
- express = sharing with other people

## Second brain = slow burn🔥 
- work at mutliple things at once
