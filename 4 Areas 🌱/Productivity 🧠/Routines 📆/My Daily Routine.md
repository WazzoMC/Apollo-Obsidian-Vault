## ✅ The Minimum Day
The few things that make a day "good enough" (the ✅ Minimum habits in [[🔁 Habits]]):
1. 🌅 Wake up early
2. 🎯 1h of deep work
3. 🌙 In bed on time

## ☀️ A normal day
| Time  | What |
| ----- | ---- |
| 7:00  | 🌅 Wake up, water, no phone for 30 min |
| 7:30  | 🍳 Breakfast |
| 8:00  | 🎯 Deep work: the most important thing first |
| 12:30 | 🍽️ Lunch + a short walk |
| 13:30 | 💼 Meetings, email, smaller tasks |
| 17:00 | 💪 Workout or a walk |
| 19:00 | 🍲 Dinner · fill in 🌙 Tomorrow's Top 3 in the daily note |
| 21:00 | 📖 Read, screens off |
| 22:30 | 🌙 In bed |

## 🗓️ The week
| Day | Extra |
| --- | ----- |
| Mon · Wed · Fri | 💪 Workout |
| Sun | 🔁 Weekly review (open the week's note) |
