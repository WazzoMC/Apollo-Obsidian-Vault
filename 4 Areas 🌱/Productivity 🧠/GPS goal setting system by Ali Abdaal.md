Goals = series of tasks/actions

GPS
1. Goal
- Be clear and specific about your goal -> numbers, outcomes, etc...
- Choose goals that are emotionally compelling enough!!
	- actually care about achieving goal
	- intrinsically motivated
- What are your anti goals?
	- constraints: no work more than 40h/week, no work in weekends

Template: goal? what? why? anitgoals?

2. Plan
	1) 3-5 major moves
		- ex: reduce visceral fat? cardio 3 times per day, calorie deficit, etcc...
	2) Realistic?
		- In Theory and Practice!!
		- keep over 80% both chance
	3) Crystal ball. If your plan failed in 3 years, why?
		- how can you make sure those bad things dont happen? how can u avoid those obstacles?

3. System
	- how will I make sure I stick to the plan?

	1) Tracking - excel, habit tracker, etc...
	2) Reminders
		- Remind yourself to look at the goal
			- weekly review
			- Daily journaling
			- time blocking (add blocks in calendar and repeat so u see them)
	3) Accountability
		- check in every week with friends
		- google sheet score card: how much u worked, how well u did, etc
