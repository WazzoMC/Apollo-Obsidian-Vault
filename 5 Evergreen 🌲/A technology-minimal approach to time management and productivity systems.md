## intro
Inspired by [[My Time Management System]], this approach adopts a technologically minimal way to productivity and task management. 

Phones are distracting. That's a truth. Constant buzzing, notifications, apps and temptations distract the mind from what it was actually made for: a predictable routine, focused on deep work. 

Therefore, choosing a route with less technology might lose you time in the moment, but win you many more minutes in the long run. No more constant pulling at your attention. Let yourself think clearly. ☁️ 🧘‍♂️ 🧠 

## the system
- Keep a calendar on a notebook or e ink tablet
- Save to do's / tasks on todoist (or a notebook, if you really wanna go technology FREE)
- Use Google Calendar (digital app) for saving meeting links, time blocking different parts of your day / life, etc...
- Every morning, check today's Google Calendar and add those time blocks to your notebook-calendar
- After that, check to do's for today / assign any to do's for today, and add them to EISENHOWER MATRIX
- Plan your day according to your tasks and time blocks on you Notebook-Calendar
