Closed ecosystems suck.

When you value portability and the ability to transfer your data through different OS's or devices, an Apple Reminders To-Do List isn't suddenly as shiny as you first might've first thought. 

Trust me. I tried using the iCloud web-app version on my Windows Laptop, and it just wasn't as nice as something like Todoist, which works across Windows, Mac, Iphone, Android, and Linux!

Don't get me wrong. I thing the Apple Reminders app is far better - the UI and its simplicity really hit home. But when performance comes at the cost of portability and freedom, I like to take a step back and see what other options there are.
