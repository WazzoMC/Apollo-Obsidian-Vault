> [!tldr] In one line
> Make the right friends, mine and fish on lucky days, use quality sprinklers, and fish or plant potatoes for early money.

## 🤝 Friends to make first
- **Pam**: give parsnips on her birthday (Spring 18) → energy tonics, battery packs, defense meals
- **Linus**: likes any food → 4♥ gives the **Wild Bait** recipe
- **Shane**: birthday Spring 20, likes beer + pizza → Pepper Poppers recipe
- **Marnie** at 2♥ → the purple shorts event 😄

## 🍀 What luck does
- More ladders, geodes, ore and coal in the mines, and fewer items lost if you die
- More treasure chests and mystery boxes, better trash can finds, more wood on the Forest farm
- It does **not** affect fish bites, fish quality or crop quality

## ⛏️ Mining
- Put a **chest by the elevator** and empty your bag every 5 floors
- You only get XP from rocks that drop something
- Tools: copper by floor 30, steel by 40, gold by 80
- Best weapon: clubs / hammers

## 🎣 Fishing
- Bamboo → fiberglass pole + bait. Fishing food (trout soup from Willy) boosts your skill
- Struggling? The **training rod** makes it easy, but you only catch basic fish
- Use short, quick clicks, and ride the bar up when the fish jumps

## 💧 Sprinklers
- Basic = cheap but many needed, **Quality = best balance** (her pick), Iridium = late game

## 💰 First-week money
- Fish at the **mountain lake**. On rainy days, catfish in the river
- Plant **potatoes** (chance of extras), later strawberries / cauliflower. Plant mixed seeds, don't toss them
- Item quests on Pierre's board pay 3× the item's value. The first museum donation gets 250g from Gunther
- Break mining nodes for gems

## 🧠 Wish-I-knew
- Sell straight to Pierre / Willy / Clint for instant cash
- Passing out after 2 AM costs at most 1,000g, so staying late in the mines is fine
- Bombs are the best way to clear your farm
