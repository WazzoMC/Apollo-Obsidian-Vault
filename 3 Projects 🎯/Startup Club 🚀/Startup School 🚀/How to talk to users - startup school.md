## Why best founders talk to users throughout the lifetime of the company 
- they will keep you honest
- build personal connections!

## How to find your users and how to talk to them
your network
- dont be afraid of rejection

coworkers
- be a bit more friendly but still respectful

people you don’t know
- linkedin, reddit, slack/disc, in person events

## What questions to ask and not ask
- deeply understand the problems, not features 
- Open ended questions

find out:
- does ur company care about X?
- why you care or not care?
- who in your company is relevant here?

ask:
- tell me how you do x today
- what is the hardest thing about doing x?
- why is it hard?
- how often do you have to do x?
- why is it important for your company to do x?
- what do you do to solve this problem for yourself?
follow ups:
- what do you mean by that?
- can you tell me more about that?
- why is that important to you?

don’t ask:
- will you use our product?
- which features would make product x better?
- yes/no questions
- how would a better product look like to you?


## How to turn conclusions into an MVP
1 Synthesizs your learnings
2 create a problem/solution hypothesis
3 start sketching an mvp

Is solving this problem valuable?
1) are people paying money for solutions in this space today?
2) have you tried to solve this problem on your own?
3) some customers will always be easier than others to do research on

## MVP prototype sessions
- don’t tell them what to do
- tell them to speak their mind as they use the app
- do a bunch of these prototype sessions and interviews
- keep these customers included and make them feel special. Create groups and communicate prototype as it’s progressing (exclusively!)
- early access to an amazing product!
