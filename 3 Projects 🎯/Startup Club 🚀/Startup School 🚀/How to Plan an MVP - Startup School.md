- MVP = the first thing you can give to the very first set of users you want to target in order to see if you can deliver any value at all to them

- Some understanding of the problem is very helpful when building an MVP so please talk to your users before starting to work on the solution.

Goal of pre-launch quickly
1. Launch something bad QUICKLY!!!
2. Get initial customers
3. Talk to customers and get feedback
	- most people think about the idea so they think the bad thing is gonna be bad anyways
	- hold the problem you're solving tightly
	- hold the customer tightly
	- hold the solution you're building loosely
4. Iterate (improve the product)

Lean MVP (in most cases)
- very fast to build (weeks not months)
- very limited functionality
- appeal to a small set of users
	- a vision of everyone
	- and a very small MVP
- this is just a starting point. It's not special. You just have to start

It's more important to get customers!
Launch = get your first customers

**Learning from customers is easier with an MVP than without**


Hacks for building an MVP:
- Time box your spec (Give yourself a deadline)
- Write your spec (Write down the features you think are required to start your MVP)
- Cut your spec (Go through each one and see if a truly desperate customer really needs that)
- Last: **Dont fall in love with your MVP!**
