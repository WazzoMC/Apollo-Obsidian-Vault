Just launch quickly, then iterate.

GOAL as an early stage founder:
- Launch MVP quickly
- Get initial customers
- Talk to customers and get feedback
- Iterate (improve the product) so you can help the customer actually solve the problem
- Rinse and repeat 

Usually, after 3-5 repetitions your MVP will be very different.

- Dont be afraid of your first customer not liking your product
- dont spend 1 year building something perfect to avoid that

- Dont be a "Fake Steve Jobs". Most people think they should just spend 1 year building an amazing product, not test and iterate, and get it perfect out.
- Even the great Steve Jobs iterated.
- The first iphone had 2g, bad screen, no video camera, a scrolling wheel which would have dirt get stuck in it
- If even he iterated, then so should u


Who are these people who actually want to use crappy MVP's?
Youre telling us theyre gonna be shipped fast, probably not gonna work that well, and we'll have to iterate a lot to actually make them good. 
- Who are these early adopters who'd wanna go through that experience?
- You wanna build your first version for customers who have their hair on fire!
![[Pasted image 20260813144128.png|315]]

Hair is on fire story:
- Your hair is on fire
- You expect water product from me to solve your problem
- I only have a brick
- Would you leave mad that I offered you that?
- No. You'd use the brick and hit your head to stop the fire


**You don't start your startup with all the answers.**
It's all about learning.
